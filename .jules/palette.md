## 2025-03-05 - Semantic Schema Context and Keyboard Accessibility of Hover Tooltips
**Learning:** When developing schemas for non-visual or utility logic nodes (like `Switch` and `Custom Combo` nodes), the lack of clear input/output and node-level descriptions leads to critical screen-reader and visual ambiguity. Users tab through input widgets and rely on contextual tooltips for screen reader feedback and hover states. Providing searchable aliases and informative, detailed tooltips is a fundamental requirement for accessible keyboard navigation and discoverability of logic components.
**Action:** Always complement core node schemas with comprehensive descriptions, clear input parameter-level tooltips, explicit output-level tooltips, and discoverable search aliases to ensure visual clarity and accessibility of logical nodes.
